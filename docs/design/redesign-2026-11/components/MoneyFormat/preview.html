Signs, zero, unknown, estimate, loading

A dash means unknown and only unknown; zero is a real zero. Colour on a number only marks direction (+ in success). Overdue amounts stay in text colour; the badge beside them says overdue.

Long amounts and long names, at 130% text

Numbers never truncate and never wrap; the words beside them give way. Every money column is designed for 12.500.000.000 ₫ at 130% text. Names clamp to two lines in cards and truncate to one line in table cells, with the full text in title.
