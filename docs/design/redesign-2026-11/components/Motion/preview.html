Nhắc tiền thuê qua Zalo

Never animate

  • Money. No counting up; a changed amount cross-fades once.
  • Table rows on sort or filter. They reorder in place.
  • Page transitions. The new page is simply there.
  • Skeleton shimmer. Skeletons hold still or pulse opacity once a second.
  • Loops other than a spinner, parallax, bounce, confetti, and typing dots for Trợ lý.
