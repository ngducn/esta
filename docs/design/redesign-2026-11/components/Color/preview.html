Surfaces

White cards on a cool light grey; dark is its own slate palette, not an inversion. In dark, raised surfaces get lighter instead of casting shadows.

Text on every ground

Body and second lines meet AAA (7:1); muted and links meet AA (4.5:1). Disabled text is exempt and always comes with a reason in words.

Borders and focus

Anything you type into or tick has a 3:1 edge; cards only need a hairline. The focus ring is brand blue in light and a lighter blue in dark, at least 3:1 on every ground.

Accent

One accent, same fill in both themes. Hover and pressed step darker in both, so the label stays well above 4.5:1.

Semantic sets

Each status has text, bg, border, solid and on-, tuned separately per theme. Light uses step 700 on 50, dark 300 on 950. Info borrows the brand blue as a tint because it never asks for attention. Colour never works alone: every status carries an icon and a word.

Ramps

Five OKLCH ramps; components never use them directly. Slate has extra steps at both ends for white cards and dark grounds.
