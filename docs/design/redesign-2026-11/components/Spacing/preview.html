Spacing

A 4px base, with 2 and 6 for the inside of small controls. Inside a component use 2–16, between components 16–32, between sections 32–48.

Radius

Tighter than the prototype's 10/12/16. Small corners read as precise; the warmth comes from photos and copy, not pillowy shapes.

Borders

Every border is a 1px hairline. border decorates; border-field (3:1) edges anything you type into or tick; 1.5px only for checkbox, radio and the completeness ring.

stroke-hairline 1px · border
stroke-hairline 1px · border-field
stroke-strong 1.5px · checkbox

Elevation

Hairline first, shadow second. Shadows mark what floats: menus, toasts, sheets. No coloured shadows, no glow. In dark, lighter surfaces do the lifting and shadows barely read.

shadow-mdMenus, toasts

Focus ring

A 2px blue outline, 2px out, on :focus-visible. It is an outline, so nothing moves when it appears; at least 3:1 on every ground in both themes.
