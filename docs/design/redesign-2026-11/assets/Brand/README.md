# Brand

The logo is **ESTA** in spaced capitals (Inter Display Medium, +22% tracking, outlined) with a small blue square at the top right of the A, like a seal on a land document. The app icon is the **E** with the same square on a tile. Inks are fixed: blue `#1b5bda` (`accent`), light blue `#689cfe` (`blue-400`, on dark only), ink `#15191f` (`slate-925`) and white.

- `esta-logo.svg` — primary: ink letters, blue square. Light grounds and the app sidebar.
- `esta-logo-reversed.svg` — white letters, light-blue square. Dark grounds, the blue panel, photos under a scrim.
- `esta-logo-mono.svg` — all ink, for print and monochrome exports.
- `esta-app-icon.svg` — white E and square on a blue tile, 22% corners (14 on 64). App icon and favicon.
- `esta-app-icon-ink.svg` — white E, light-blue square on an ink tile. Dark contexts and the collapsed rail.

The square is 24% of the cap height, 0.05em from the A, top-aligned to the cap line. Clear space is the cap height on every side. Minimum sizes: wordmark 12px tall, app icon 16px. Never retype the name in live text as a logo, move or drop the square, recolour, or add effects.
